## Arms and measured labels

**A — conditional admission, expert, strength.** Pool the width-12 trunk over source, protected, and editable regions, concatenate normalized codec/QP, and predict separate source-level admission, four-expert mixture, and strength. A hard admission threshold of .5 makes a rejected stream exact source identity. Empty editable support returns identity. Training uses the differentiable gate, editable RGB and positive expert/strength losses, and class-balanced admission BCE with coefficient .05. Only positive rows supervise expert and strength; identity rows directly supervise admission and RGB identity. Actual marginal-byte utility remains capped at two. The same support gain and hard-core restoration apply to profiles and neural renders. The 12 original V29-A profiles are measured again in the V30 implementation and form A's bank.

**B — A plus eight fresh measured profiles.** Keep A's 12 baseline profiles and add four pure experts at strength .20 and four 50:50 mixtures: mild-Gaussian/block-lowpass and strong-Gaussian/background-DC, each at .75 and 1.00. The canonical target has four mixture weights summing to one and a strength; pure profiles are one-hot and mixed profiles have two .5 entries. Render new RGB, encode both codecs, and run the unchanged teachers. Never infer candidate bytes or guards from another profile. B's 20-profile bank is an audit comparison, while its primary selector still sees only three neural proposals plus the original controls.

**C — A with one task-context expert replacement.** Keep four expert slots and a separate, freshly measured 12-profile bank. Replace block-lowpass with a causal AR temporal low-pass on stationary editable RGB, resetting recurrence at scene cuts and attenuating displacement by `(1-motion)^2`. For single-frame OD, replace it with background chroma smoothing that preserves weighted source luma: subtract the luma projection from Gaussian sigma-8 displacement and use the largest in-gamut scalar before applying the common support gain. Preserve hard semantic cores exactly. C receives no temporal OD features. Its mixed neural outputs and lossy decoded streams do not inherit the standalone OD expert's luma guarantee.

For every arm, a source/codec/QP label is positive only when a freshly measured profile passes the original teacher and byte guards, saves at least 1% of anchor bytes, and **strictly** beats the selected original control. Identity is a valid target. New pixels require new encode and teacher observations. Keep complete provenance, source ID and pixel hash separation, and all grid conditions, including identity. Ground truth, DEV metrics, and independent evaluator features do not enter fitting. Freeze all configs, thresholds, profile parameters, and checkpoint selection before reading V30 DEV outcomes.

## Research grounding

[Lu et al., TCSVT 2024](https://doi.org/10.1109/TCSVT.2024.3441049) motivates QP conditioning and true-codec observations; its BPG proxy gradient and training budget differ from V30 RGB distillation. [Pang et al., ICIP 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.08849) motivates measuring filter strengths and types against the actual codec, but optimizes perceptual quality under CBR. [Task-Switchable Pre-Processor, TCSVT 2024](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10379180) supports retaining task context rather than hard masking. [GOP-Based Deep Preprocessing, PCS 2024](https://doi.org/10.1109/PCS60826.2024.10566387) highlights temporal/proxy mismatch. These papers do not establish V30's frozen-analyzer Top1/mAP gains or the four BD-rate targets. V30 is pre-only standard RGB; it adds neither a learned codec proxy nor a receiver network/semantic side channel.
