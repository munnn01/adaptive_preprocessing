## Plan self-review and rulings

- Spec coverage: action families1/3; transport2; source disjointness1/4; calibration5; oracle/static7; selector8; runtime9; quality/CI6/9; resumable orchestration10/11; separate GOP12; release13.
- Contracts use packet total bytes throughout; all OD guard/evaluator coordinates originate from the same transform adapter. Measurements are policy-independent; policies determine supervision after calibration, preventing cache invalidation from silently changing training labels.
- Fractional FPS is exact where source metadata permits it. Unknown timing is explicitly non-qualifying instead of the legacy silent25FPS assumption; this is a fail-closed resolution of the approved temporal/rate constraints.
- Primary AR rate aggregation uses total bits/total duration, not an unweighted mean of per-source bitrates. OD uses total bits/total original pixels. Both remain paired under bootstrap.
- No plan step changes old five-QP constants. New CLI paths prevent accidental legacy runner defaults.
- K=3 primary has anchor+three proposals only. Expanded static K3 is the principal adaptive comparator; legacy K3 and controls remain additional diagnostics.
- GOP settings restrict latency symmetrically; they are an independent audit and never qualify preprocessor headroom.
- Candidate-unit failures are marked unavailable and fall back; systemic missing source timing/codec failure stops stage qualification. Unavailable slots are not fabricated as valid measured successes.
- Review Focus tests belong to their owning tasks; no extra dependency or unrelated refactor is introduced.
