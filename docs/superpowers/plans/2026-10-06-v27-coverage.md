## Global constraints

- TRAIN128 contains all ten groups per source; source-blocked folds and no DEV/TEST.
- Teacher guard .1/strict anchor, minimum byte saving .01, K3 unchanged.
- All coded bytes include headers; exact integer equality means zero gain.
- V25/V26 pixels and checkpoints remain reproducible.
- True learned credit requires conditional proposal origin at that point.

## Review focus

- Missing/wrong anchor reconstruction must fail before new pixels render.
- Portfolio ties/zero utilities must produce three distinct deterministic actions.
- Repeated codec/QP records of a source must share folds without scaler leakage.
- Complete TRAIN grid must be measured, not synthesized from other QPs.
- Component-oracle results must not influence primary learned proposals.
